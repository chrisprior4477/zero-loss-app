## Thirty-second Undo Entry checkpoint — September 21

- Owner's positive password-confirmed $1 funding check is reconciled: one authorization, one succeeded $1 funding session, one $1 ledger credit; Playable Balance $195. No extra funding was added by this inspection.
- Approved workflow: reserve the entire 1–10-ticket submission for 30 server-timed seconds; Undo releases that whole reservation to the same Playable Balance, not to the card. Final entries and their outcomes/shares are created only after the deadline. This is the owner's previously approved accidental-entry policy, not a refund of an accepted entry or a deposit.
- `public.entry_requests` implements the canonical pre-Entry workflow (`validating`, `accepted`, `cancelled`, `rejected`). Snapshot fixture outcomes stay private; owner-readable audit events use `entry.requested`, `entry.accepted`, `entry.cancelled`, `entry.rejected`. Prior entries and outcomes remain immutable and untouched.
- Ledger-owned hold/release interface derives integer cents from immutable request terms. Reservation and release each have unique request identities, composite wallet ownership, and append-only history. Finalization replaces the hold with ordinary per-entry debits atomically; no new balance authority is introduced.
- Reserved slots count in ordinary availability. Existing repeatable TV/shoes/baby scenarios are preserved. Account/offer eligibility is checked again at acceptance; a changed restriction rejects/releases the reservation. Public legacy RPCs cannot bypass the window once enabled. Repeated requests for the same pending prize resume that request, even with a new page-generated key.
- Site-wide navy/cyan Undo notice survives navigation/reload by re-reading Supabase, supports batches and truthful network errors, and offers exact-result links after acceptance. Wallet history/notifications distinguish holds, hold releases, and final purchases. Browser timers are display-only and do not authorize value changes.
- Private server worker uses a non-overlapping job and pre-acquires customer locks before offering locks, skipping busy customers to avoid cross-customer lock inversions. [Supabase Cron](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/cron/quickstart) supports the five-second scheduler; server completion does not require an open browser.

## Provider research (September 21, 2026)

- GiftCard Partners supports recipient delivery and provider-generated claim links, depending on product eligibility. Preserve one order identifier across retries; a timeout must not create a second order with a new identifier. [Orders](https://developers.giftcardpartners.com/docs/orders), [Delivery types](https://developers.giftcardpartners.com/docs/delivery-types).
- Fulfillment can be asynchronous. Treat callbacks as retryable signals and reconcile order status, not as permission to issue another reward. [Webhooks](https://developers.giftcardpartners.com/docs/webhooks).
- Stage credentials and its test-balance behavior need confirmation from the provider before actual stage orders. No provider orders were placed in this audit. [Sandbox](https://developers.giftcardpartners.com/docs/sandbox).
- Stripe illustrates why refunds need their own durable state: original payment-method destination, partial-refund limits, pending/failure states, and dispute interaction. This informs the proposed design; it does not set Zero Loss's refund entitlement policy. [Refund documentation](https://docs.stripe.com/refunds).
