# Zero Loss email copy — owner review

These are proposed messages, not active emails. For each number, reply **yes**, **no**, or **change**. Words in `{braces}` are filled from the customer's account and the specific prize; buttons open the signed-in website. No gift-card number or code appears in email.

**Proposed sending rules:** combine a win and an immediately ready reward into one message; if issuance takes longer, send #1 and then #8. Send purchase-option reminders only when an option first opens, at 7 days left, and at 24 hours left—not every interval currently stored. Never email Crew members about a win unless the winner chooses to share it and the recipient allows that type of update. Do not send old queued messages when a new worker is first enabled.

**Standard demo footer for prize, entry, wallet, and reward messages:** “Zero Loss is an experimental MVP. Entries, payments, and rewards in this preview are simulated.” Preference-based emails also include a link to manage that preference.

## Entry results and purchase options

### 1. You won

**Decision:** ☐ Yes ☐ Change ☐ No  
**Subject:** You won: {prize name}  
**Body:** Congratulations—your entry for {prize name} was selected. Your prize is a {gift-card value} {retailer} digital gift card. If your reward is ready, sign in to view and claim it. If it is still being prepared, we will let you know when it is ready. You never need to pay to receive a prize you won.  
**Button:** View my reward

### 2. Paid entry not selected; purchase option available

**Decision:** ☐ Yes ☐ Change ☐ No  
**Subject:** Your result is ready: {prize name}  
**Body:** Your {entry amount} entry for {prize name} was not selected. You have an optional purchase option: pay the remaining {remaining amount} to receive a {gift-card value} {retailer} digital gift card. Your entry amount is already reflected in that remaining price. The choice ends {deadline, Eastern Time}. Each entry stands alone; amounts from separate entries do not combine. You do not have to buy anything else.  
**Button:** Review my purchase option

### 3. Free postcard entry not selected; purchase option available

**Decision:** ☐ Yes ☐ Change ☐ No  
**Subject:** Your free-entry result is ready: {prize name}  
**Body:** Your free entry for {prize name} was not selected. You may choose to purchase a {gift-card value} {retailer} digital gift card for {full gift-card price} until {deadline, Eastern Time}. Because this entry was free, no entry payment is applied to the purchase price. This is optional; your free entry is complete whether or not you buy.  
**Button:** Review my option

### 4. Purchase-option deadline reminder

**Decision:** ☐ Yes ☐ Change ☐ No  
**Subject:** Your {retailer} purchase option ends {relative deadline}  
**Body:** Your optional purchase option for {prize name} is still available. The remaining price is {remaining amount} for a {gift-card value} {retailer} digital gift card. It ends {deadline, Eastern Time}. If you want it, review the details before the deadline. If not, there is nothing you need to do.  
**Button:** Review my option  
**Suggested timing:** 7 days and 24 hours before expiry only; skip a reminder if the option was purchased, declined, expired, or the customer turned reminders off.

### 5. Declined-option reminder, requested by the customer

**Decision:** ☐ Yes ☐ Change ☐ No  
**Subject:** The offer you declined expires in two days  
**Body:** You asked us to remind you about your declined {retailer} purchase option for {prize name}. You can still revive it until {deadline, Eastern Time}. Revival is optional; if you are not interested, you can ignore this email.  
**Button:** Review declined offers

## Friends and Crew

### 6. Crew invitation

**Decision:** ☐ Yes ☐ Change ☐ No  
**Subject:** {sender name} invited you to their Zero Loss Crew  
**Body:** {sender name} sent you a private Crew request. You can approve or decline it in Zero Loss. Nothing from your activity is shared with them until you approve the connection and choose your sharing settings. If you do not recognize this request, you can ignore it.  
**Button:** Review Crew request  
**Rule:** Send only for a verified, existing Zero Loss account with a real pending request; do not reveal whether any other email address has an account.

### 7. A friend shares a prize

**Decision:** ☐ Yes ☐ Change ☐ No  
**Subject:** {sender name} shared a prize with you  
**Body:** {sender name} thought you might like {prize name}. Take a look at the prize details and decide for yourself whether to enter. Sharing a prize does not reserve an entry or change either person's chances.  
**Button:** View the prize  
**Rule:** Send only to the specific approved Crew members the sender selected; never to sample profiles.

### 8. A friend shares a win

**Decision:** ☐ Yes ☐ Change ☐ No  
**Subject:** {sender name} shared an update with their Crew  
**Body:** {sender name} chose to share a win for {prize name} with you. You can view the shared activity in Zero Loss. We do not include gift-card numbers, account balances, or private entry details in this email.  
**Button:** View shared update  
**Rule:** This is off by default and requires the winner's explicit share choice and the recipient's notification preference.

## Rewards and favorites

### 9. Reward ready to view or claim

**Decision:** ☐ Yes ☐ Change ☐ No  
**Subject:** Your {retailer} reward is ready  
**Body:** Your {gift-card value} {retailer} digital gift card for {prize name} is ready in Gift Cards & Rewards. Sign in to review its status and any claim deadline. For your security, the gift-card number is available only inside your account.  
**Button:** Open my reward  
**Rule:** Do not send separately when #1 already says the reward is ready; use this if reward issuance finishes later or after a purchase option is completed.

### 10. Reward claim deadline

**Decision:** ☐ Yes ☐ Change ☐ No  
**Subject:** Claim your {retailer} reward by {claim date}  
**Body:** Your {gift-card value} {retailer} reward is waiting in your Zero Loss account. Its claim deadline is {claim date, Eastern Time}. Please sign in and check the reward details before then. If you already claimed it, no action is needed.  
**Button:** View my reward  
**Suggested timing:** 7 days and 1 day before an actual claim deadline, only while the reward remains claimable.

### 11. Favorite reaches 90% full

**Decision:** ☐ Yes ☐ Change ☐ No  
**Subject:** {prize name} is almost full  
**Body:** A prize you asked us to watch has reached {percent}% full. {remaining count} of {total entries} entries remained when we checked. Availability may change before you open the page, and this alert does not reserve an entry.  
**Button:** View saved prize  
**Rule:** One email per watched prize, only while the customer has this alert enabled.

## Account and service

### 12. Verify a new account

**Decision:** ☐ Yes ☐ Change ☐ No  
**Subject:** Confirm your Zero Loss email address  
**Body:** Thanks for creating a Zero Loss account. Confirm this email address to finish setting it up. If you did not sign up, you can ignore this message.  
**Button:** Confirm my email  
**Rule:** The button is a one-time account-security link. Do not include prize or promotional copy.

### 13. Reset password

**Decision:** ☐ Yes ☐ Change ☐ No  
**Subject:** Reset your Zero Loss password  
**Body:** We received a request to reset the password for this email address. Use the button below to choose a new password. If you did not request this, you can ignore this message; your current password will not change.  
**Button:** Reset password  
**Rule:** The button is a one-time account-security link. Do not include prize or promotional copy.

### 14. Approved free postcard-entry confirmation

**Decision:** ☐ Yes ☐ Change ☐ No  
**Subject:** Your free entry was approved: {prize name}  
**Body:** We received and validated your free postcard request for {prize name}. Your entry is now recorded under {entry reference}. You can use that reference if you contact us about this entry. We will post its result in your account when the pool is complete.  
**Button:** View entry details  
**Rule:** Only after staff validation creates an actual entry; never merely because a postcard was mailed or received.

### 15. Support case reply

**Decision:** ☐ Yes ☐ Change ☐ No  
**Subject:** There is a reply to your Zero Loss support case {case number}  
**Body:** Our support team replied to your case about “{case subject}.” Sign in to read the reply and continue the private conversation. For privacy, the full message is not included in this email.  
**Button:** Open my case  
**Rule:** Send only for a new staff reply to the case owner, not for the customer's own messages.

## Optional additions to decide separately

### 16. Product request received

**Decision:** ☐ Yes ☐ Change ☐ No  
**Subject:** We received your product suggestion  
**Body:** Thanks for suggesting {product name} for Zero Loss. We have your request and may follow up if we need more detail. This is not a promise that the product will be listed.  
**Button:** Browse prizes  
**Rule:** Only if the product-request form is connected to a durable request record.

### 17. Entry or wallet transaction receipt

**Decision:** ☐ Yes ☐ Change ☐ No  
**Subject:** Your Zero Loss demo activity is recorded  
**Body:** Your {activity type} for {prize name or wallet} is recorded in your account. Amount: {amount}. Reference: {reference}. Review My Activity for the full details. This preview uses simulated funds and entries; this message is not a receipt for a real payment.  
**Button:** View My Activity  
**Rule:** This is not currently promised by the site; enable only if a separate receipt is wanted. Do not email during the Undo window or before the transaction is final.

### 18. Business/creator-service update

**Decision:** ☐ Yes ☐ Change ☐ No  
**Subject:** Zero Loss creator services are now available  
**Body:** You asked us to let you know when expanded promotional and creator services became available. They are ready to explore. See the details and decide whether they fit your business.  
**Button:** Explore business services  
**Rule:** This is a future announcement, not a current transactional email. Send only after the business application and opt-in are actually stored, the service launches, and an unsubscribe method is available.
