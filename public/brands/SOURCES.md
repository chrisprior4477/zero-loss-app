# Wallet button marks

- Apple mark: https://www.apple.com/ (global navigation SVG, retrieved October 25, 2026). The SVG canvas is fitted to the unchanged mark and displayed in white on the dark wallet control.
- Google G: https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png (retrieved October 25, 2026; unchanged).

The preview buttons remain unavailable until wallet pass URLs are connected. These marks identify their destinations; they do not represent a payment integration.
